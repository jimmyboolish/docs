### **An Important Note on Sybil Detection & Provisional Allocations**

A core commitment of this distribution is to the long-term health of the Caldera network. To that end, please be aware of the following:

- **Allocations are Provisional:** The amounts shown on the pre-claim site are **provisional** and subject to change based on the results of the final Sybil detection analysis.
- **Sybil Detection:** A rigorous on-chain analysis has been completed to identify and flag Sybil clusters (groups of addresses likely controlled by a single entity). To ensure a fair distribution, allocations for addresses flagged by these criteria may be significantly adjusted.

This process is a necessary measure to ensure that the governance and utility of \$ERA rests with the broadest and most authentic group of community members possible.
