The total supply of \$ERA is fixed at **1,000,000,000 tokens**. The distribution and unlock schedule are designed to empower the community and ensure the long-term, sustainable growth of the Caldera protocol.

## Distribution Philosophy

The \$ERA distribution is designed around a core principle: the value of the network should belong to those who build, secure, and use it. The allocation model prioritizes long-term alignment and rewards genuine participants who are invested in the future of the Internet of Chains.

## Token Unlock Schedule

The token release schedule is designed with specific mechanisms for each category to ensure network stability and long-term alignment.

### Summary of Key Unlock Terms

- **Core Team & Early Backers/Investors:** Subject to a 1-year lockup (cliff). At the 12-month mark, 15% of their total allocation unlocks, with the rest unlocking linearly over the subsequent 24 months (concluding at month 36).
- **Foundation & R&D:** 25% of the total allocation for each category unlocks at TGE, with the remaining 75% unlocking linearly over 48 months.
- **Community Treasury:** 25% of its total allocation unlocks at TGE, with the remaining 75% unlocking linearly over 48 months.
- **Airdrop:** 50% of the total allocation unlocks at TGE, with the remaining 50% unlocking linearly over 4 months.

**Staking Locked Tokens:** Please note that tokens subject to a lockup or vesting schedule can still be staked to help secure the network. Any staking rewards earned on these tokens will be subject to the same unlock schedule as the principal tokens.
