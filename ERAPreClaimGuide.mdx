The pre-claim is a **mandatory step** for all eligible participants to secure their token allocation. It is designed as a protective measure to ensure a fair and secure distribution that rewards the genuine community members who have contributed to the Caldera ecosystem.

**Pre-Claim Window:** Open now until **Tuesday, July 15th, at 9:00 AM PST**.

## A Community-First Philosophy

Traditional airdrops have often fallen short of their goal to foster long-term alignment, becoming targets for mercenary capital and Sybil farmers who extract value from the ecosystem.

The \$ERA distribution is founded on a different principle: to reward the builders, users, and supporters who share the long-term vision for a unified Ethereum. To honor this, a robust analysis was implemented to identify authentic participants. This pre-claim process is a deliberate and transparent step towards community ownership, ensuring that \$ERA empowers those who are truly invested in the future of the Internet of Chains.

## \$ERA Allocation Eligibility

The criteria were designed to reward a wide range of genuine participants, including active users of Caldera-powered rollups, members of key partner communities, and other valuable contributors across the broader rollup ecosystem. Eligibility for onchain activity of Caldera users was determined by a snapshot taken on March 5th, 2024 - with activiy from other criteria rolling through pre-claims.

These include:

- Caldera-powered Chains Users
- Caldera Community Members
- Ape/BAYC Community
- Manta Community
- Modular Ecosystem
- Public Goods Developers
- Kaito Yappers
- Ecosystem Projects
- General L2 Users

## How to Pre-Claim: Step-by-Step Instructions

Follow these steps carefully to secure the token allocation. The entire process should take only a few minutes.

### **Step 1: Visit the Official Portal & Prioritize Security**

The only official, secure pre-claim website is: [**https://claim.caldera.foundation**](https://claim.caldera.foundation).

**SECURITY WARNING**

**Verify the URL.** Bookmark `https://claim.caldera.foundation` and always confirm you are on the correct site before connecting a wallet.

**Official accounts will never contact you first.** Anyone sending a direct message, asking for personal information, or requesting funds is a scammer.

**This is a gas-free process.** You will **never** be asked to send funds or pay gas fees to pre-claim tokens.

### Step 2: Connect Wallet

Connect the wallet that was used to interact with the Caldera ecosystem.

### **Step 3: Complete Proof of Humanity Verification**

Immediately after connecting your wallet, a one-time **Proof of Humanity** verification with partner Intract is required. This is a crucial security step to prevent bot activity and ensure tokens are distributed fairly to genuine community members.

### ****Step 4: Check for AdditionalRewards (Optional)****

If you are an open-source developer or an active community contributor (e.g., on X), you may be eligible for additional rewards. Use the integrated tools on the page to safely connect your GitHub and/or X accounts to check for these bonuses.

### **Step 5: View Allocation, Understand Bonus, and Confirm by Signing**

After completing the previous steps, the portal will display your provisional total allocation. This allocation will also include a bonus.

- **Bonus Portion:** Each recipient will recieve a **bonus** which will be **automatically staked for 90 days**. This is a required component of the claim process to create long-term alignment and help secure the network from day one.
- **Finalize by Signing:** To finalize the pre-claim, you will be prompted to **sign a message** with your wallet. This action reserves your full allocation and is **completely gas-free**.

Congratulations\! The pre-claim process is now complete.
