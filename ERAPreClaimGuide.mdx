## How to Pre-Claim: Step-by-Step Instructions

Follow these steps carefully to secure the token allocation. The entire process should take only a few minutes.

### **Step 1: Visit the Official Portal & Prioritize Security**

The only official, secure pre-claim website is: [**https://claim.caldera.foundation**](https://claim.caldera.foundation).

**SECURITY WARNING**

**Verify the URL.** Bookmark `https://claim.caldera.foundation` and always confirm you are on the correct site before connecting a wallet.

**Official accounts will never contact you first.** Anyone sending a direct message, asking for personal information, or requesting funds is a scammer.

**This is a gas-free process.** You will **never** be asked to send funds or pay gas fees to pre-claim tokens.

### **Step 2: Connect Wallet & View Provisional Allocation**

Connect the wallet that was used to interact with the Caldera ecosystem. The portal will then display a **provisional allocation** based on the on-chain snapshot. A breakdown of the qualifying activities will be visible on-screen.

### **Step 3: Check for Developer Rewards (Optional)**

Open-source developers may be eligible for additional contributor rewards. Use the integrated GitHub tool on the page to safely check eligibility.

### **Step 4: Complete Proof of Humanity Verification**

To ensure tokens are distributed to genuine community members and to prevent bot activity, a one-time **Proof of Humanity** verification with partner Intract is required. This is a crucial security step that protects the integrity of the distribution for all participants.

### **Step 5: Confirm Pre-Claim & Consider the Staking Bonus**

On the final page, there is an **optional** opportunity to pre-stake the allocation for a 90-day period. In exchange for helping to secure the network from day one, participants who choose this option will receive a **staking bonus** of \$ERA tokens.

To finalize the process, click "Reserve" or "Confirm" to submit a **gas-free** claim reservation.

Congratulations\! The pre-claim process is now complete.
